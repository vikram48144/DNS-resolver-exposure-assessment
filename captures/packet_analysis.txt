SAFETY AND SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------

The assessment was performed only against designated DNS servers
inside the controlled Docker laboratory.

Controls:
- No Internet-wide scanning
- No spoofed source traffic
- No amplification testing
- No DNS stress testing
- Low query rate
- Maximum target limit enforced
- Controlled authoritative test zone
- Explicit retry and timeout limits
- Packet-level evidence retained for reproducibility

The assessment methodology is designed around safe DNS resolver
exposure measurement and the open-recursive-resolver concerns
described by RFC 5358.
